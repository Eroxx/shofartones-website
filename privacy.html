Privacy

Updated October 4, 2026

Shofar Tones collects nothing. It has no account, no analytics, no ads, and it never sends anything over the internet. Everything you make stays on your iPad.

What it stores

Your settings, your presets, your patterns and your MIDI key choices, saved on your iPad. Inside a music app, they're saved in that app's project, like any other instrument's settings. Deleting the app deletes them.

What it sends

Nothing. The app makes no network requests. The horn sound, the room recordings and the photographs all ship inside the app.

MIDI

The app listens to MIDI keyboards and controllers connected to your iPad so you can play it. That MIDI never leaves your iPad.

Links

A few buttons in Settings open web pages in your browser: this site, the credits' source pages, and my other apps on the App Store. Those pages have their own privacy policies.
